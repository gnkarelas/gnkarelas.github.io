About

I am a PhD candidate at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona, advised by Gábor Lugosi, and a predoctoral researcher at the Barcelona School of Economics, funded by an FPI fellowship from the Spanish Ministry of Science.

I work in discrete probability and probabilistic combinatorics, and most of my questions come from game theory. Lately that means random games: when two players follow best-response dynamics in a game whose payoffs are drawn at random, how often do they find a pure Nash equilibrium, and what do the cycles they get trapped in look like?

Before Barcelona I studied mathematics at the University of Patras and at KTH and Stockholm University, and completed an M.Sc. in data-driven computing at Patras.

Research

My interests are discrete probability, game theory, random graphs and probabilistic combinatorics. Papers are also listed on Google Scholar.

Preprints

Cycle structure of best-response dynamics in random bimatrix games

with Gábor Lugosi and Rui-Ray Zhang

2026. Under review.

We study the cycles of the best-response digraph of a bimatrix game with random payoffs: how many there are, how long they get, and how the answer changes with the payoff structure.
